Caylee Cowan arrived in Venice for her upcoming film Company, and apparently she wasn't planning on letting anyone else have any of the red-carpet attention, but really she just can't help being so incredibly captivating.

The actress and producer looked absolutely stunning as she stepped onto the red carpet at the Venice Film Festival for the premiere of Company, the new film written and directed by her longtime boyfriend, Casey Affleck. Caylee (our dream girl) is also part of the film's cast, making this one very much a personal and professional moment for the couple.
