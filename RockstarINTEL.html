Rockstar Quietly Releases GTA Online Update To Fix Glitches

Rockstar Games have released a new background patch for GTA Online according to dataminer Tex2. As part of the recent Event Week which contains Halloween content, Rockstar made a background update involving some of the seasonal

The company have also continued to respond to various issues which came as part of this summer’s GTA Online DLC. The Kortz Center Heist has had a bunch of bugs which Rockstar are still squashing. A way to get access to modded cars has also been patched.

Background updates from Rockstar fix issues without the need of downloading an update. It is all handled server side. This is a routine occurrence after a new GTA Online update so nothing out of the ordinary here.

Patch Notes

The recent background update patch notes for October 7th have been shared by TexFunz2:

  • Fixed an issue that resulted in players being displaced when using the Nightclub office computer
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in zombies spawning inside buildings during the Cayo Perico Survival
  • Updated the Stealth Annihilator fix during The Kortz Center Heist Finale to avoid interfering with its scripted flight playback
  • Patched “Invisible Indestructible + Weapons” RC Bandito glitch
  • Patched “Director Mode Online Car Drop” glitch
  • Patched “Invisible & Off the Radar Casino Penthouse” glitch

Were you impacted by any of these issues? Let us know down in the comments.

There’s always a chance Rockstar issue more background updates in the days and weeks to come to stay tuned to Tex’s feed and RockstarINTEL for the latest GTA Online news.

These fixes are separate from the absolutely massive list of squashed bugs and improvements that came in patch 1.73 Kortz Center Heist update. You can read the full patch notes for yourself here.

    GTA Online Event Week: Halloween Event Continues (October 8th-14th)

    By Aaron TruemanOctober 7, 2026 Event Week

    A new GTA Online Weekly Update has begun and the month long event Halloween event continues with new and old spooks plus rewards. Rockstar have also rotated various content around and have big bonuses. Check out everything happening in this GTA Online during this Event Week which will run from October 8th through 14th.

    Halloween Rewards

    • Get the Twilight Knife T-Shirt for free through October 7th (must be claimed from in-game clothing stores)
    • Complete five Payphone Hits through October 14 to receive GTA$500,000 within 72 hours of completion
    • Complete all Weekly Challenges through November 4 to receive GTA$2,000,000 within 72 hours of completion
    • GTA+ Members can also claim a free Pegassi Oppressor motorcycle with the Skulls livery, along with additional Halloween treats and more.

    The FIB Priority File in GTA Online this week is The Black Box File. Priority Files pays double.

    This week at the Casino in GTA Online, take your daily spin with the lucky wheel for your chance to win the Annis Savestra, which cost  $990,000 from Legendary Motorsport. GTA+ members now get 2 spins per 24 hours.

    Prize Ride Challenge

    This week in GTA Online, place top 4 in a LS Car Meet Race for 2 days in a row to unlock the Declasse Walton L35 as the Prize Ride vehicle. It costs $1,670,000 from Southern San Andreas Super Autos.

    Hao Premium Test Ride Vehicle

    Test Track Vehicles

    This week’s GTA Online Test Track Vehicles are the Declasse Vamos, Dewbauchee Seven-70 & Ocelot Locust.

    Luxury Autos Car Dealership

    The GTA Online Luxury Autos Car Dealership located in Rockford Hills has two cars on show for purchase. The cars this week are the Dewbauchee Rapid GT X & Übermacht Sentinel XS4.

    Premium Deluxe Motorsport

    Simeon’s Auto Shop has five cars available to purchase and test drive in GTA Online. They are the Albany Brigham, Albany Fränken Stange, Albany Lurcher, Declasse Tornado Rat Rod & LCC Sanctus.
