The Portal is the newest entry into the 900 Series line for 900 Global. This ball uses the new Conduit AI asymmetrical weight block, which gives it a high RG (2.59″) and a large differential (0.050″). In addition to the new core, the Portal uses a new solid coverstock. The Reserve Blend 92 Solid cover is finished with Power Edge, providing more length than the Vengeance, Vengeance Returns, and Viking Conquest. This ball has a skid/flip shape that cleared the front part of the lane very easily. We needed some friction for this ball to be at its best, and the fresh medium oil pattern is where all three of our testers had their best reactions.

Tweener

Tweener liked the strong move at the breakpoint the Portal made for him on the fresh medium oil condition. He was able to play a little straighter up the lane than he could with the Vengeance Returns on the fresh, using the strong back end motion and big move off the spot to get the ball into the pocket. He saw very little transition or change in the pattern when it was only the Portal going down the lane. The ball didn’t do much to beat up the front or the track area on its own. It wasn’t until duller, more aggressive balls went down the lane that he had to start making his adjustments.
