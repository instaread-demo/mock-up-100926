Drummond Seeks Return to Bogalusa City Council

Former councilman pledges to focus on public safety, quality of life and putting Bogalusa’s best interests first.

SPECIAL TO THE DAILY NEWS

My name is Teddy Drummond, and I am seeking one of the two Council at Large seats for the City of Bogalusa in the upcoming November 3rd election. I’d like to take this time to ask for your vote and support, as well as tell you a little about myself.

I’m a lifelong resident of Bogalusa and have been married to the same wonderful woman for 35 years. We have two sons, and I retired from the Bogalusa Paper Mill after almost 32 years of service. I am a proud Catholic, and I also attend Superior Avenue Baptist Church with my wife, where I’m treated like family.

Having served on the council in the past, I’ve always been open, honest and upfront with the public about all matters. If elected, this city’s best interest will be my top priority, just as it was when I served on the council in the past.

I have made contacts throughout the state that I’ve kept in touch with and continue to call on to help Bogalusa.

My Pledge to the Citizens of Bogalusa

• Help promote Bogalusa any time the chance arises.

• Help improve the quality of life for all the citizens of Bogalusa.

• Help promote public safety. When citizens don’t feel safe, nothing else matters.

• Help maintain a civil atmosphere at City Council meetings so that citizens can be heard in a civil manner and city business can be conducted as well.

• Always put the best interest of this city first and foremost.

I humbly ask for your vote on November 3rd, and my promise to you is that I will work hard and make you proud that you chose Teddy Drummond for one of your two votes for Council at Large.
