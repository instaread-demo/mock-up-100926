Planning permission sought for new water bottling facility in Offaly

By David Power

Multimedia reporter

Published 9th Oct 2026, 14:55 BST

Planning permission is being sought from Offaly Co Council for a new two-storey water-bottling facility.

A planning application has been submitted for a proposed new two-storey water bottling facility at Clashawaun, Clara.

This will include a raw materials store, finished products store, packing and unpacking areas, canteen, clean room/bottling area, small laboratory, various office areas and welfare facilities, loading and unloading area, bike shed and bin storage facility, vehicular access together with all associated site development works.

The application was submitted by Clara Water Ltd .
