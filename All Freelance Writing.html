Poetry Submissions: Get Paid to Write Poetry

August 24, 2026 by Jennifer Mattern

Listen to audio version of this article

Most freelance writing markets promoted here revolve around nonfiction writing. But plenty of outlets are interested in your more creative writing as well -- such as poetry submissions.

These are all paying poetry markets, though rates vary. Browse their summaries below, then visit the links to access each market's full writers' guidelines.

Like other market lists at All Freelance Writing, be sure to bookmark this post and check back often if you're looking to become a published poet. Because my market lists connect to the site's larger writers' market database, they update automatically any time an outdated listing changes or gets removed, and whenever new markets fitting their criteria get added.

Whether you're a new poet or someone who has written poetry for years and is seeking new outlets for publication, I hope this list gives you some new ideas for future poetry submissions. And remember, you can click the "markets" link at the top of any page to browse the full writers' market directory.

Markets that Pay for Poetry Submissions

Abyss & Apex

Abyss & Apex is a magazine specializing in speculative fiction. They accept submissions only during open reading periods. They accept flash fiction (up to 1000 words) and short stories (up to 10,000 words). The publication pays $.08 per word for up to 1000 words and an $80 flat payment for longer works. The magazine also accepts poetry.

AGNI

AGNI accepts stories, essays, and poems that are previously-unpublished. There are no word limits "though space is at a premium and length sometimes affects decisions." They do not publish romance, horror, mystery, or science fiction but are open to fiction borrowing elements of them. They don't publish academic essays or purely journalistic pieces, and they pay $30 per printed page for accepted prose and $50 per page for accepted poetry, up to a maximum of $300. Submissions are considered for both print and online publication. Free submissions may be made via mail.

Analog

This magazine features science fiction stories, art and poetry as well as some fact based articles. Pay is 8-10 cents per word for short stories up to 20,000 words, 6 cents per word for serials of 40,000-80,000 words, and 9 cents per word for "fact articles" of around 4000 words. Payment is $1 per line for poetry.

Asimov's Science Fiction

Asimov's is a magazine for science fiction writers. They pay 8-10 cents per word for stories up to 7500 words and 8 cents per word for for each word over 7500 words (though they rarely buy stories over 20,000 words or shorter than 1000 words). Poems up to 40 lines are also accepted and pay is $1 per line.

Contemporary Verse 2

Contemporary Verse 2 accepts poetry, interviews, articles, essays, reviews, French-language poetry, and translations during recurring reading periods. Poetry pays CAD $35 per poem; print interviews, articles, and essays pay CAD $75-150; print reviews pay CAD $65-100; and web-only interviews, essays, and reviews pay CAD $65-150. Unsolicited overseas contributors outside Canada and the United States whose fee is CAD $40 or less receive contributor copies instead of cash, except Canadians abroad using a Canadian mailing address.

Grain Magazine

Grain Magazine accepts poetry up to six pages; fiction and literary nonfiction up to 3500 words; and short plays, comics, translations, and experimental or hybrid work up to 3500 words or five visual pages. It also accepts rolling pitches for 450-word book reviews, 900-word interviews, and 900-word craft essays. All writing pays CAD $50 per printed page up to CAD $250, plus two contributor copies.

MAYDAY Magazine

MAYDAY Magazine publishes fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, translation, cultural commentary, interviews, and reviews. Pay is $20 for book reviews/interviews, creative nonfiction, culture, fiction, and translated stories, $50 for MAYDAY:Black, $10 for poetry and translated poems; culture pieces run about 600-6,000 words.

Poetry

Poetry accepts original and unpublished poems in English, or translations into English. They only accept previously-unpublished submissions. They pay $500 per poem for text and visual poems, $600 for video poems, and $400 per published page for prose. Submissions are closed from June 15 - September 15 each year.

Prairie Fire

Prairie Fire accepts one general submission per year in each of three categories: poetry, fiction, and creative nonfiction. Poetry submissions may include up to five poems, while fiction and creative nonfiction may be up to 10,000 words. Print poetry pays CAD $40 per poem; prose pays CAD $0.10 per word, with a CAD $250 maximum for fiction, essays, articles, and other creative non-fiction. They also accept plays and scripts with a maximum payment of CAD $50 as well as interviews and profiles.

PRISM international

PRISM international accepts fiction, creative nonfiction, comics, hybrid work, literary essays, poetry, and authorized translations. Prose is preferred at 4000 words or less, and poetry submissions may include up to four poems totaling six pages. Prose pays CAD $40 per printed page and poetry pays CAD $45 per printed page.

Rattle

Rattle accepts unsolicited poetry submissions year-round. They do not accept work previously published in books, magazines, or similar curated collections, but posting on the author's own blog, message boards, or social media accounts is not disqualifying. They encourage simultaneous submissions. Pay is $200 per poem and a one-year subscription if the poem is published in print, and $100 for online contributors.

Scientific American

Scientific American accepts articles on "recent scientific discoveries, technical innovations and overviews of ongoing research." They prefer submissions from scientists with first-hand knowledge in their field or experienced science journalists. Feature articles typically run 2000-4000 words. Opinion and analysis articles run around 1000 words. They also accept poetry.

The Georgia Review

The Georgia Review accepts literary fiction, essays, poetry, and reviews of books, visual media, artistic events, and exhibits from August 15-May 15. Fiction and essays are rarely published at 9000 words or longer, reviews must be at least 1200 words, and poetry submissions may include up to 10 pages. Print prose pays $50 per printed page, while poetry pays $4 per line up to $800.

The Maine Organic Farmer & Gardener

The Maine Organic Farmer & Gardener publishes organic agriculture, gardening, sustainable living, home energy, and small-farm features. It accepts pitches and pays 25 cents per word on publication for assigned articles. Features generally run 1200-2200 words. How-to articles run 500-1200 words. Interviews run up to 2500 words. Reviews run 300-500 words. Readers' stories / essays can run up to 500 words. They also accept poetry and pay $20 per published poem.

The Offing

The Offing is an online literary magazine that accepts essays, fiction, and poems. For 2026, guidelines state there are no submission fees, and contributors are paid $25 to $100 depending on department and length.

The Sun Magazine

The Sun publishes essays, fiction, and poetry. Payment for all submission types are $200+.

The Threepenny Review

The Threepenny Review accepts critical articles dealing with books, film, theater, and art exhibits(1200-2500 words), Table Talk items (1000 words or less), stories and memoirs (4000 words or less), and poetry (100 lines or less). They pay $400 per story or article, and $200 per poem or Table Talk piece. Note: Reading windows are only open from Jan 1st - April 14th.

U.S. Catholic Magazine

U.S. Catholic accepts freelance submissions across a wide variety of magazine sections including features, reviews, poetry, essays, and different columns throughout the publication.
